# Chapter 5 — Win the Boardroom Before the Next Incident

## Chapter job
Equip leaders to turn the book's risk analysis and proposed actions into credible executive decisions—not a technology pitch.

## Argument spine
1. Express risk in service-provider consequences: continuity, customer impact, regulatory exposure, reputation, and trust of authorities.
2. Connect investment to operator economics, legacy transformation, supplier dependence, and availability expectations.
3. Give the board choices: what to fund, what to prioritize, what risk remains, and who owns it.
4. Establish an executive review cadence so AI-era security remains a resilience decision, not a one-off initiative.

## Evidence and examples to develop
- Build a concise board-level case from sourced operator obligations and clearly stated assumptions.
- Use an illustrative decision scenario only when labeled as such; never invent a real board quote or incident.
- Make the language understandable to non-specialists without minimizing uncertainty.

## Reader decision
Can the CISO/CTO make the case for the next 12 months of work in terms the CEO and board can decide?
