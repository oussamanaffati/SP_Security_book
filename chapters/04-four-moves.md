# Chapter 4 — Four Moves Every Service Provider Should Start in the Next 12 Months

## Chapter job
Turn the preceding risk analysis into a concrete, sequenced 12-month agenda for an SP CISO/CTO. Each move should have an accountable sponsor, a first action, and evidence of progress.

## Four moves
1. **Establish a view of consequential AI and agent use.** Sponsor: CISO/CTO jointly with business and operations leaders. First action: commission a cross-functional review of agents, owners, data, permissions, tools, and suppliers, prioritizing network operations, OSS/BSS, and sensitive data. Progress evidence: an agreed, owned priority register.
2. **Reduce excessive access before scaling deployment.** Sponsor: identity/security and platform owners. First action: identify high-impact agent permissions and define approval, least-access, human-authorization, and revocation expectations. Progress evidence: named owners and an approved control policy for priority use cases.
3. **Rehearse AI-accelerated incidents without endangering service.** Sponsor: security operations with network operations and continuity leadership. First action: run a tabletop for agent misuse, supplier compromise, or disruption to critical functions. Progress evidence: decision gaps, escalation owners, and tracked remediation actions.
4. **Secure executive sponsorship for a funded roadmap.** Sponsor: CISO/CTO, with CEO/board decision. First action: present prioritized risks, owners, milestones, residual risk, and requested investment. Progress evidence: explicit executive decisions and a 12-month review cadence.

## Chapter guardrails
- Sequence work to fit operator change windows, regulatory duties, budget cycles, and service-continuity requirements.
- Avoid implying that a policy or inventory alone secures an agent or guarantees resilience.
- Keep measures tied to risk reduction and operational readiness, not tool deployment counts.

## Reader decision
Which three or four moves will this operator launch, who owns each one, and what will the board review in 12 months?
